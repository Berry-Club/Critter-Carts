**Scoochstem** and **Scoochstem Wood** are travel surfaces used by Scoochworms. Connected paths can run horizontally, climb walls, cross ceilings, and wrap around corners. A wandering Scoochworm that reaches either block begins following the path.

Both variants behave as logs: they can be placed along any axis, burn, and are mined most effectively with an axe. Regular Scoochstem has exposed ends, while Scoochstem Wood is bark-covered on every face.

The creature travels on the exposed faces of the blocks rather than through their centers, so leave enough room around bends for its body. At a branch, a Scoochworm prefers to continue forward. If the forward path ends, it can turn left or right. When both choices are valid, the choice is random.

## Disabling Faces

Use an **Axe** on a Scoochstem to toggle the clicked face. A disabled face cannot support Scoochworm travel and uses a darker texture.

Each of the block's six faces is controlled independently. This prevents travel on unwanted sides without requiring the block to be removed.
