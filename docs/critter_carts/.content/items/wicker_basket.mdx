The **Wicker Basket** is a 27-slot Scoochworm attachment intended for automatic item collection and unloading.

When the Scoochworm runs over dropped items, it inserts them into its Wicker Baskets in body order. Items that do not fit remain in the world.

A Wicker Basket cannot be accessed manually. When its segment travels upside down along a ceiling, the basket drops items beneath itself. By default it drops one item every 2 ticks.

Sneak and interact with an empty hand to remove the basket. Its contents remain inside the item when it is removed or dropped.
