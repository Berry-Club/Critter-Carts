The **Saddlebag** is an attachment that gives one Scoochworm segment a freely accessible 9-slot inventory.

Use it on an empty segment to equip it, then interact with that segment to open the inventory. Sneak and interact with an empty hand to remove the Saddlebag. Its contents remain inside the item when it is removed or dropped.
