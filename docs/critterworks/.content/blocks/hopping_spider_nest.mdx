The **Hopping Spider Nest** sends out four tiny spiders to carry items between inventories connected by [Web Lines](@critterworks:web_lines).

Connect the nest itself to the web network, then install [Spider Nest Interfaces](@critterworks:spider_nest_interface) at the inventories that should supply or receive items. The spiders automatically choose available work, collect up to one stack at a time, and deliver it. A spider can accept more work from its current node instead of returning to the nest between deliveries.

Multiple nests can share the same web network. Their spiders coordinate with one another so they do not try to collect from or deliver to the same place at once.

## Spider Jobs

A job begins when the network has an item in an **Input** inventory and room for it in a matching **Output** inventory. Both interfaces must use the same color, allow the item through their filters, and have a complete Web Line route connecting them to the nest.

When several jobs are available, spiders prefer the highest Input priority, followed by the highest Output priority. The nest then prefers a spider that is not already transporting anything, choosing whichever available spider has the shortest Web Line route to the Input. If the remaining choices are equal, it prefers the job that can move the most items.

Each spider reserves its pickup and delivery while working so another spider does not choose the same job. It travels from its current node to the Input, collects up to one stack, carries it to the Output, and then becomes available for another job while returning home. If the Output fills while the spider is traveling, it waits there and tries again. If the job becomes invalid because an interface, color, or filter changed, the spider returns the item to its original inventory before going home.

## Idle Wandering

Spiders without transport work wander around the connected web network. They usually move to a neighboring node, avoid immediately retracing their last line when another route is available, and occasionally choose a more distant node. A spider may pause briefly after reaching a node, and individual spiders move at slightly different speeds.

At night, idle spiders make their way back to the nest and remain nearby. Transport work takes priority over wandering, but a wandering spider finishes crossing its current Web Line before accepting a job so it never jumps between points on the network.

## Checking the Spiders

Interact with the nest to view each spider's position and current behavior. This screen shows whether it is wandering as well as when a delivery is waiting or can no longer be completed.

If the nest is broken while a spider is carrying something, the carried item is dropped at the nest.
